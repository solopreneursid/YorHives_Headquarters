# Products — Evidence-backed commercial shortlist (2026-11-21)

This is **founder research**, not an autonomous product launch instruction. These projects came from private YorpHivesHQ discovery signals and were cross-checked against their public GitHub metadata on November 21, 2026. GitHub stars and Hacker News points are public attention indicators, **not verified visits, signups, sales or paying customers**. None meets the new 3-observation meaningful multi-day growth test at this snapshot. Customer willingness to pay, acquisition costs and build costs have not been verified.

### Founder decision recommendation

**Investigate career-ops category first**, because GovRadar could be a product-adjacent launch channel, and a browser-first CV-to-job checklist has a smaller initial scope than a general agent platform. Before building, check that GovRadar has real demand, that job sources and candidate data are handled lawfully, that the original repository license is understood, and that any paid functionality is differentiated. **Do not assume this opportunity is a profitable product because the reference repository has stars.**

### Additional watchlist and risks

[T3 Code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code) (MIT), [bex](https://github.com/bex-co/bex) (Apache-2.0), and [Arcane](https://github.com/getarcaneapp/arcane) (BSD-3-Clause) are useful technical and market references. Full coding-agent products and infrastructure platforms carry substantial compatibility, support and security obligations. They should not automatically outrank simpler user-facing tools merely due to public star counts.

HQ must continue to monitor and recommend only. Repositories, production deployments, external websites and commercial purchases remain founder-authorized operations. No new navigation tabs, paid APIs, browser evasion or new self-improving execution privileges are needed.
